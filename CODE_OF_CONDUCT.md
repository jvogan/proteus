# Code Of Conduct

Proteus follows a simple standard: be direct, respectful, and constructive.

## Expected Behavior

- Assume good intent and focus on the technical issue.
- Be specific when reporting bugs or proposing workflow improvements.
- Respect different levels of biology, tooling, and agent experience.
- Avoid harassment, personal attacks, discrimination, and private-data exposure.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove comments and contributions that make the
project unsafe or unproductive. Repeated or severe violations may result in
being blocked from the project.
