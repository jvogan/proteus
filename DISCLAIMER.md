# Disclaimer

Proteus is research and education tooling for structural biology workflows. It
helps AI coding agents call tools such as PyMOL, ChimeraX, AlphaFold DB, RCSB
PDB, UniProt, and Rosetta-oriented workflows from the terminal.

Proteus is not a medical device, clinical decision support system, diagnostic
tool, or substitute for expert review. Predictions, confidence metrics, docking
views, validation summaries, and rendered structures can be incomplete or
misleading. Use independent validation and domain expertise before making
scientific, medical, regulatory, or business decisions.

Respect the licenses and terms of any external tools, databases, model weights,
and services you use with Proteus.
